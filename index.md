## Ideas for Future Projects

I plan to revisit the [AI Snake Game](/pages/ai-snake-game.html) using [Textual](https://textual.textualize.io/) as a framework. I found this short article by Richard Sutton, [The Bitter Lesson](/pages/bitter-lesson.html). In my previous stab at developing a high performing AI agent to play the game, I fell into the trap that is described by Richard. His article has inspired me to take a different approach to surpass the plateau I hit with my previous implementation i.e. a score of about 100 on a 20x20 board. That's no small feat, but I could do significantly better, given a slow game pace. My goal is to create an agent that outperforms me. I think having a database of patterns to augment the choices that the agent makes may allow me to break through that high score.
